EmberSpark is a small, open-source Android client for your own self-hosted LNbits server, for sending and receiving Bitcoin over the Lightning Network. It is not a wallet on its own. EmberSpark works like a remote control that lets you use your LNbits server as a wallet from your phone, wherever you are. The app never holds private keys or seed phrases. It only stores the API key your LNbits server gave you, encrypted in the Android Keystore.

EmberSpark itself holds nothing. Your sats live on the LNbits server you operate, which runs on hardware you control or trust. The app is purely a UI client. There is no EmberSpark backend, no centralised accounts, no exchange, no token sale, no on-device mining. Private keys live on your LNbits server and never reach the app.

WHAT IT DOES

* Connect to your LNbits server and send or receive Bitcoin Lightning payments through it.
* Pay BOLT11 invoices, BOLT12 offers, LNURL-pay links, and Lightning addresses (user@host) routed through your server.
* Receive via BOLT11 with a QR code generated by your LNbits server. Share your server's Lightning address, BOLT12 offer, or LNURL-pay link via QR, copy, system share, or NFC.
* Auto-generate LNURL-pay links and BOLT12 offers on your LNbits server.
* Receive by LNURL-withdraw: scan or tap a withdraw voucher or Bolt Card to pull sats into your server.
* Log in to websites that support LNURL-auth, signed on the device. No password, no email.
* Scan QR codes, paste destinations, decode QR images from your gallery, or read Lightning data from NFC tags.
* Tap any amount to swap between sats and your preferred fiat. USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, CAD, AUD, JPY via mempool.space.
* Save contacts you pay regularly. Edit and delete entries. Each contact shows the same recognisable avatar across devices and across users.
* Manage multiple LNbits server connections in one app. Switch the active connection by swiping the home avatar up or down, reorder your connections, and keep a separate identifier for each.
* Add a connection with a read-only invoice key. It is marked read-only and can receive but not send.

BOLT CARDS

* Set up and manage Bolt Cards (NXP NTAG424) through your LNbits server's Boltcards extension.
* Add a card, write it over NFC, verify it works, and reset it, all from the app. No separate card-programming app needed.
* Tap a programmed Bolt Card to your phone to pull its sats into your server.

PRIVACY

* No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
* No EmberSpark servers. The app talks to your LNbits server, mempool.space for exchange rates, and GitHub for update checks (not in the F-Droid build). That is the full list.
* The API key for your LNbits server lives in the Android Keystore and never leaves the device.
* Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are deliberately disabled. Re-enter your LNbits server URL and API key on a new device.
* Optional biometric or device-PIN lock on app launch.
* Optional notifications for received payments, off by default. Turning them on asks for the notification permission and checks your balance periodically in the background.

REQUIREMENTS

* A self-hosted LNbits server with a real Lightning funding source (LND, CLN, Phoenixd, or NWC).
* An admin or invoice API key from your LNbits server. Scan the QR from LNbits or paste it once at setup.
* Recommended: the LNURLp extension on your LNbits server, for LNURL-pay links and Lightning addresses. Optional but recommended.
* Optional: the Boltcards extension on your LNbits server, plus a phone with NFC, for Bolt Card features.

LANGUAGES
